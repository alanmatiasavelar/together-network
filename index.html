The idea

A social network built around niches, not noise — where the feed is made of real projects and real goals. Anyone can create one, and anyone can join one: individuals, third‑sector organizations, private companies, and government or public bodies.

Two ways in

Start something, or join something.

Every project on Together has people building it and people backing it. You can be either — often both, on different projects.

Start a project

Post the idea, set the goal, and open it up.

  • Create a project and put it on the feed
  • Set goals and requirements for collaborators
  • Invite people, and review who wants in
  • Manage the team and the budget as it grows
  • Track progress until the goal is reached

Join a project

Find something worth your time or your money.

  • Browse a feed built from real projects, not posts
  • Search for projects that match your skills or values
  • Subscribe and send a proposal to join
  • Contribute work, funding, or both
  • Get paid, or get credit — your call going in

How a project moves

From idea to finished, in five stages.

1

Create

Post the project and set the goal.

2

Recruit

Invite collaborators, open it to supporters and sponsors.

3

Work

Coordinate in shared tools spun up per task.

4

Track

Watch progress against the goal, adjust as you go.

5

Finish

Hit the goal, close the project, keep the credit.
